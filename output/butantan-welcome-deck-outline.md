### Slide 3: Proven Innovation — Eque-cel (CT103A)

**Bullet Points:**

- Clinical results: **ORR 98.9%**, **CR/sCR 88.4%**, **MRD negativity 98.9%** — among the best in class globally
- Superior safety profile: Grade 3+ CRS only 1.0%, zero neurotoxicity, zero MDS/AML
- Expanding into **autoimmune diseases** (myasthenia gravis, MS, SLE) and **earlier treatment lines** (2L/3L, frontline high-risk MM)

## 3. Narrative Logic

The 6-slide structure follows a **hospitality-to-vision arc** designed specifically for a first partner meeting:

| Slide | Purpose | Trust-Building Role |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| 1. Welcome | Establish emotional tone | "We are honored to host you" — signals respect |
| 2. At a Glance | Establish credibility quickly | Earns the right to discuss collaboration |
| 3. Eque-cel | Prove execution capability | Concrete data validates the company's claims |
| 4. Platforms | Show depth and breadth | Signals IASO is a technology ecosystem, not a single product |
| 5. Global Vision | Reveal strategic alignment | Brazil on the map creates an organic "aha moment" |
| 6. Looking Forward | Open invitation to dialogue | Positions collaboration as mutual, not one-sided |

**Why this works for a partner visit:**

Trust in a first meeting is built through **warmth → demonstrated capability → shared vision → open invitation**. Each slide advances this sequence. The deck is deliberately short to leave maximum time for dialogue — which is where real partnerships begin.

The Brazil connection on Slide 5 is shown naturally within IASO's global strategy, rather than as a dedicated "why we want to work with you" slide. This avoids being transactional while still creating alignment. The CEO's speaker notes provide the verbal bridge to make this connection explicit.

## 4. Suggested Improvements

### 1. Personal Touch — CEO's Opening Connection

Add a brief personal anecdote or connection at the opening. For example:

> "I've long admired Butantan's role in making advanced therapies accessible to millions across Brazil and Latin America."

This takes 15 seconds but creates genuine warmth that a generic welcome cannot.

### 2. Leave-Behind One-Pager

Prepare a **single-page handout** (not the full deck) summarizing:

- IASO snapshot (key numbers)
- Key pipeline highlights
- 3 collaboration models (clinical partnership, technology transfer, manufacturing support)
- Contact information and QR code linking to the company website

This gives Butantan something concrete to share internally when they return to São Paulo.

### 3. Mid-Presentation Dialogue Moment

Before Slide 6, the CEO could pause and say:

> "Before I close, I'd love to hear a few words from your side — what brought you to Nanjing, and what aspects of cell therapy are most interesting to Butantan?"

This transforms the presentation from a monologue into a conversation midstream, which is far more engaging for international visitors who may feel politely obligated to wait until the end. It signals genuine curiosity and respect.
