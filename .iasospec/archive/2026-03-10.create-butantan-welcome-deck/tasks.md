## 3. Narrative Logic

The 6-slide structure follows a **hospitality-to-vision arc** designed specifically for a first partner meeting:

1. **Slide 1 (Welcome)** — Establishes emotional tone. The CEO positions IASO as a gracious host, not a seller. Mentioning "listening and learning" signals respect.

2. **Slides 2–3 (Credibility)** — Quickly establishes that IASO is a serious, proven company. The CEO earns the right to discuss collaboration by showing execution capability (commercialized product, market leadership, strong clinical data).

3. **Slide 4 (Depth)** — Shifts from "what we've done" to "what we can do." Platforms signal that IASO is a technology ecosystem, not a single-product company. This is where Butantan's R&D expertise finds natural connection points.

4. **Slide 5 (Strategic Alignment)** — The global strategy slide creates an organic "aha moment" when Brazil appears on the map. Instead of saying "we want to work with you," the slide shows that Brazil is already part of IASO's strategic thinking. This is more compelling and less transactional.

5. **Slide 6 (Invitation)** — Closes with an open, forward-looking invitation. The explicit mention of "listening" and "exploring" avoids the trap of a one-directional pitch. The CEO positions collaboration as mutual, not one-sided.

**Why this works for a partner visit:** Trust in a first meeting is built through warmth → demonstrated capability → shared vision → open invitation. Each slide advances this sequence. The deck is deliberately short to leave maximum time for dialogue — which is where real partnerships begin.

## 4. Suggested Improvements

### Improvement 1: Personal Touch — CEO's Connection to the Visit

Add a brief personal anecdote or connection at the opening. For example: "I've long admired Butantan's role in making advanced therapies accessible to millions across Brazil and Latin America" or reference a specific Butantan achievement. This takes 15 seconds but creates genuine warmth that a generic welcome cannot.

### Improvement 2: Leave-Behind One-Pager

Prepare a single-page handout (not the full deck) summarizing: IASO snapshot, key pipeline, 3 collaboration models (clinical partnership, technology transfer, manufacturing support), and contact information. This gives Butantan something concrete to share internally when they return. Include QR codes linking to the company website and a brief video if available.

### Improvement 3: Invite a Butantan Moment

Before Slide 6, the CEO could pause and say: "Before I close, I'd love to hear a few words from your side — what brought you to Nanjing, and what aspects of cell therapy are most interesting to Butantan?" This transforms the presentation from a monologue into a conversation midstream, which is far more engaging for international visitors who may feel politely obligated to wait until the end. It signals genuine curiosity and respect.
