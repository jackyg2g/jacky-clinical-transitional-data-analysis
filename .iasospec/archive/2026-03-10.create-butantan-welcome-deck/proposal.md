# Proposal: Butantan Institute Welcome Presentation Deck

## Requirement Summary

Design a concise 5–6 slide welcome presentation for visitors from Butantan Institute (Brazil), to be delivered by the CEO in approximately 5–8 minutes, introducing IASO Biotherapeutics and creating an open atmosphere for partnership discussion.

## Background and Motivation

- IASO Biotherapeutics (驯鹿生物) will host visitors from **Butantan Institute**, one of the largest biomedical research and vaccine production centers in the Southern Hemisphere, based in São Paulo, Brazil
- This is an **exploratory first meeting** — no existing formal relationship
- IASO's global strategy explicitly includes **Brazil as a bridging study target** for Eque-cel (alongside Japan), making this visit strategically significant
- A well-designed welcome deck sets the tone for productive partnership discussions and demonstrates IASO's readiness for international collaboration
- The presentation must balance professionalism with warmth — it is a welcome, not a sales pitch

## Goals and Success Criteria

- Welcome the Butantan team and create a collaborative atmosphere
- Provide a clear, high-level overview of IASO's capabilities and positioning
- Highlight mutual areas of potential collaboration (especially cell therapy access in Latin America)

**Success Criteria**:
- Deck is 5–6 slides, suitable for 5–8 minutes
- Narrative is tailored for an international biomedical partner (not a generic investor deck)
- Speaker notes are written for the CEO
- Collaboration opportunities are concrete and relevant to Butantan's capabilities

## Scope and Boundaries

### In Scope (Included This Time)

- Presentation outline (slide titles, bullet points, suggested visuals)
- Speaker notes for each slide (CEO voice)
- Narrative flow rationale
- Improvement suggestions for international engagement

### Out of Scope (Not Included This Time)

- Actual PowerPoint/Gamma file creation — outline only
- Detailed clinical data slides — keep high-level
- Financial or investor-oriented content — this is a partnership meeting
- Chinese language version — English only for this meeting

## User/System Scenarios

### Scenario 1: CEO Delivers Welcome Presentation

- **Who**: CEO (张金华)
- **When/Condition**: Start of Butantan Institute visit, conference room setting
- **What**: 5–8 minute introduction using 5–6 slides, followed by open discussion
- **Result**: Butantan team has a clear understanding of IASO's mission, capabilities, and collaboration potential; atmosphere is warm and open

### Scenario 2: Deck Shared Post-Meeting

- **Who**: Butantan delegation
- **When/Condition**: After the visit, reviewing materials internally
- **What**: Reference the deck to brief colleagues on IASO's profile
- **Result**: Key messages (global CAR-T leader, Brazil bridging interest, manufacturing capability) are self-evident from the slides

## Constraints and Assumptions

### Constraints

- English language only
- 5–6 slides maximum, 5–8 minutes delivery time
- Professional but warm tone — welcome, not sales pitch
- Executive-level clarity — avoid technical overload
- Audience knows the biotech industry but not IASO specifically

### Assumptions

- Butantan's interest is likely in cell therapy technology transfer, manufacturing partnership, or clinical collaboration in Brazil
- CEO will present in person with opportunity for Q&A afterward
- Existing analyst deck (Cure_AP_Deck_Consol_v11.pdf) provides source material but must not be simply summarized — content must be reframed for a partner welcome context
