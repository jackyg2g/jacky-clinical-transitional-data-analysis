# CAR-T/Live WBC (%) Trend Chart

Line chart showing CAR-T cell percentage of live white blood cells over time post-infusion for 76 patients in the CT103AC004 clinical trial.

- **X-axis**: Time post-infusion (BL, D5, D8, D11, D15, D22, D29)
- **Y-axis**: CAR-T/Live WBC (%)
- **Individual lines**: Each colored line with markers represents one patient
- **Dashed black line**: Median value across all patients at each timepoint
- **General trend**: CAR-T cells peak around D11-D15 post-infusion, then decline through D29
- **Peak range**: Individual Cmax values range from ~1% to ~90%, with a median of ~28%
- **Median peak**: Occurs at D11, reaching approximately 25%
