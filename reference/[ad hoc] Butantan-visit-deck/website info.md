## 1. Company Overview

**Founded:** 2017 (March, established in the PRC as a limited liability company)

**Sector:** Advanced Therapy Medicinal Product (ATMP) — one of the few commercial-stage biopharmaceutical companies in the ATMP sector bringing China's breakthrough innovations to the global market.

**Focus Areas:**
- Cell therapies and biologics for **hematological malignancies** and **autoimmune diseases**

**Core Capabilities:**
- Industry-leading, fully integrated platforms leveraging cutting-edge technologies spanning the entire product lifecycle — from discovery and development to manufacturing and commercialization.

**Pipeline Summary:**
- Ten products and product candidates in total
- Anchored by core product: **Equecabtagene Autoleucel (eque-cel)**, branded as **Fucaso®**
- Eque-cel approved in China for treatment of relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (r/r MM) in patients who have progressed after at least three prior lines of therapy
- Also being developed for earlier lines of r/r MM and autoimmune diseases (MG, MS, SLE/LN)
- Beyond eque-cel: nine additional therapeutic candidates:
  - Three autologous chimeric antigen receptor T-cell (CAR-T) therapies
  - Five in vivo CAR-T cell therapies
  - One antibody product candidate

**Key Collaborators:**
- Innovent Biologics
- Sana Biotechnology
- Cabaletta Bio
- Umoja Biopharma
- GC Cell (Korea)
- Tongji Hospital (TJH)
- Top-tier academic institutions and hospitals

**Tagline / Vision:** "Focusing on innovative biotherapies to bring hope of cure to patients"

**Mission:** "Making innovative therapies the backbone therapy to cure patients"

**Social Media:** LinkedIn, WeChat, i-connection platform

## 5. Technology Platforms

IASO Bio has five integrated technology platforms:

### 5.1 Fully Human Antibody Discovery Platform
- Built upon a fully human antibody library containing **over 240 billion antibodies**
- Offers extensive diversity and flexibility for screening optimal candidates
- Includes a fully synthetic antibody library improving antibody druggability (stability, manufacturability, binding specificity)
- Fully human single-domain antibody library advancing development of next-generation therapies (bispecific CAR-T cells, bi- and multi-specific antibodies)
- Approximately **20 internal R&D programs** initiated to date, with **four candidates advancing to clinical stages**
- Validates an efficient discovery-to-CAR-T engine that shortens development cycles, reduces program risks

### 5.2 High-throughput CAR-T Cell Therapy Screening and Optimization Platform
- Combines state-of-the-art single-cell analysis and next-generation sequencing
- Enables high-throughput screening for rapid evaluation of large pool of CAR-T lead candidates
- Evaluation based on key phenotypic characteristics and functional metrics
- Advanced analytical capabilities drastically reduce screening time

### 5.3 In Vivo CAR-T Platform
- **Enhanced Targeting:** Genetic engineering of viral vector surface proteins improves selective T cell transduction in vivo, minimizing off-target risks
- **Cell-Specific Expression:** T cell-specific promoters restrict CAR expression to intended cell type, improving safety
- **Immune Evasion:** Proprietary envelope modifications prevent elimination of viral vector by macrophages and T cells
- **Scalable Production:** Suspension-adapted packaging cell lines and advanced chromatographic purification boost yield and quality of viral vectors for clinical applications

### 5.4 Advanced Manufacturing and CMC Platform
- Led by experienced management team with deep expertise in biologics R&D, manufacturing, and quality control
- Highly efficient and tightly controlled, full-spectrum manufacturing and quality system
- Supports transition from early-stage development to commercial-scale production
- Includes development of a "China solution" for global expansion
- Encompasses: plasmid development and production, lentiviral vector development and production (ex vivo and in vivo), and the distinctive **FAST CAR-T platform**

### 5.5 Clinical Development and Clinical Translational Research Platform
- Joint laboratory established with a leading top-tier Chinese hospital
- Focus on exploring potential new targets and investigating novel mechanisms of action for first-in-class drugs
- Strong clinical network and translational expertise for efficient bridging from preclinical to clinical application
- Integrated approach significantly shortens development timelines

## 6. Manufacturing

**Overview:** Mature and stable CMC platform technology enabling large-scale, high-quality manufacturing.

### Manufacturing Modules

#### Plasmid
- Mature and stable fermentation and purification technologies for Escherichia coli
- In-process analytics monitor parameters: supercoiled ratio, impurity profile, endotoxin levels (injectable-grade standards)
- Final products sterilized through 0.22-μm filtration, filled in Class A environments, stored under validated conditions
- Internal plasmid production enhances vertical integration and supply reliability

#### Lentiviral Vector
- Internally developed platform: closed-system, automated, and scalable production
- Rigorous release testing: identity, potency, sterility, residual DNA, host-cell protein assays
- Meets global quality and regulatory standards
- High automation and scalability reduces unit cost and production variability

#### Cells
- Rapid cell product process development for GMP-compliant manufacturing
- Systematic platform for sequence screening, research and manufacturing of GMP grade plasmids, viral vectors and cells — with cost advantage
- Quality of cell products is stable and controllable; safety and efficacy superior to similar products

#### Quality
- **QC:** Technology platforms covering molecular detection, cell functions, and flow cytometry; independent analytical method development and verification; data integrity management
- **QA:** Most advanced quality control system in China, expanded scope for CAR-T specifics

### Commercialization Facilities

#### Nanjing Facility
- Total GFA of approximately **7,866 square meters**
- Full-cycle production capabilities covering plasmids, viral vectors, and CAR-T cells
- In-house analytical and quality control capabilities
- Granted Drug Manufacturing License in February 2022

#### Shanghai Facility
- GFA of **32,306 square meters**
- Located in the Shanghai Waigaoqiao Free Trade Pilot Zone
- Team dedicated to R&D and commercialization of cell and gene therapy drugs and antibody drugs

### Four Global R&D and Manufacturing Sites
1. **Global R&D Center in Shanghai**
2. **GMP Manufacturing Site in Nanjing**
3. **R&D Center in the U.S.**
4. **Research and Clinical Manufacturing Site in Nanjing**
